## What the project is

SME DRHP drafting system (capstone Track A): QLoRA fine-tune of **Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct** (student) on rows built from a
**Qwen3-32B-AWQ** teacher, then DPO against looping. Two skills: **drafting** (one sub-section from FACTS) and
**extraction** (typed facts as JSON). Prompt formats are in `data/prompts.py` and are shared by training and the app.

Data: 1,446 train rows (657 drafting) and 139 test rows (89 drafting) in `trainset/out/`; 233 DPO pairs in
`drive/out/dpo_pairs.jsonl`.
